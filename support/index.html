Contact support

Email soren@friis.io. To help us help you quickly, please include:

  • the app name and version, from the Extension Management page in Business Central;
  • your Business Central version and whether it is a production or sandbox environment;
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • any error message, and a screenshot if possible.

Please don't send personal data or business data from Business Central unless we ask for it.
