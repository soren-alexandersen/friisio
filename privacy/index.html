Our Business Central apps

Our apps run inside your own Business Central environment, which Microsoft hosts. Any data you enter in the apps, such as item hints, is stored in that environment.

  • Friis.io does not receive, access or store data from your Business Central environment.
  • Our apps do not send telemetry or usage data to Friis.io.
  • Microsoft processes your Business Central data under its own terms. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Support requests

If you contact us, we use your name, email address and the content of your message to answer you. We keep this correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and to meet our accounting obligations.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of personal data we hold about you, and you can object to our processing of it. Email soren@friis.io. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet.

Changes

If we change how we handle personal data, for example by adding telemetry to an app, we will update this page and the date above.
